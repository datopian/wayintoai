Way Into AI is my personal take on what's happening in AI and what to do about it. I'm [Rufus Pollock](/authors/rufus-pollock).

## How it started

I've been thinking about AI since 2016, and seriously since ChatGPT came out at the end of 2022. In autumn 2025 I realised I needed to get properly on top of it. I started collecting material, trying tools and writing notes, at first just for myself. Then I decided to do it in public.

This site is that open notebook. The [daily log](/logs) is what I'm finding, the [posts](/posts) are what I think, and the [weekly email](/#subscribe) is the edit: the few things that mattered, and one thing worth trying.

## What it is

- **Personal and opinionated.** It's my selection and my take. Sometimes colleagues from [Life Itself](https://lifeitself.org/) and [Datopian](https://www.datopian.com/) contribute, and they get credited when they do.
- **From practice.** I use these tools every day, especially coding agents, and write about what actually works.
- **Both sides.** [Using AI](/use) and [understanding what it's changing](/understand): work, power, policy and safety.

## What it isn't

Not neutral news, not comprehensive, not a firehose, and never sponsored.

## About me

I founded Datopian and co-founded Life Itself, and earlier I founded the [Open Knowledge Foundation](https://okfn.org/). More on my [profile](/authors/rufus-pollock) and [personal site](https://rufuspollock.com/).

This might grow into something bigger than one person. For now, it's me.
