## [[situational-awareness-ai-progress-slow-takeover|Situational Awareness — AI Progress and the Possibility of a Slow Takeoff]] — June 2024

Paul Christiano et al. forecasting paper arguing for taking "slow takeoff" seriously: AI systems improve gradually and at some point become capable enough to pose serious risk, but the transition from "deployable" to "dangerous" is gradual. Key themes: situational awareness (AI understanding its own position and acting strategically), post-training improvements (RLHF, scaling, chain-of-thought) as the main driver, autonomous replication as a warning sign.
