# BRAG

## Overview

`/brag` reads the current project, finds a product angle, storyboards a 15–25 second launch video, and hands the composition to [Hyperframes](https://hyperframes.heygen.com/) for rendering. The output includes the video, a poster frame, and suggested share copy; music and sound effects are on by default, while voiceover is opt-in.

It supports Claude Code directly and exposes the skill at the standard discovery paths used by Codex, opencode, and other agents. The repository includes several tone presets, while still allowing free-form directions such as “fake Series A launch from 2016.”

> **Not tried yet (2026-09-18):** This looks interesting, especially as a way to turn the final “show what you built” step into a reusable skill, but this entry is a bookmark rather than a hands-on recommendation.
