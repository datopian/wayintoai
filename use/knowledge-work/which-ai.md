**Why Claude, if you're unsure?** Not because it's the strongest model; that changes month to month and is debatable. It's the app. Most knowledge workers won't hit its limits. If you write a lot of code or use AI all day, you may want both.

**What about the others?** There are plenty of decent models, such as Google's Gemini and open models like DeepSeek, Qwen and Mistral. Claude and ChatGPT are the two leading frontier models and the easiest place to start.

**Do I need to learn prompting?** No. Just talk to it, as you would to a capable colleague: say what you want, give it the background, and push back when it's wrong.

This is advice for everyday knowledge work, not a general verdict on which AI is best.
