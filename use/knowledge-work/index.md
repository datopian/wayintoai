How to move your everyday knowledge work (planning, writing, coordinating with others) onto AI. Written for people on the edge of using AI or starting from zero, and drawn from how we work at [Life Itself](https://lifeitself.org). No technical background needed.

Read in order:

1. [Post-its and filing cabinets: how to organise shared work →](/use/knowledge-work/concepts) — how to organise documents and tasks so you and your AI can find them, explained with an old-fashioned office.
2. [AI is the interface: how to use AI for knowledge work →](/use/knowledge-work/how-to-use-ai) — a short manifesto, then the day-to-day practice: what's on today, turning a mess into a plan, keeping progress up to date.

This is a first version, published to be improved in public. More chapters (AI for planning, design, research and automation) will follow.
